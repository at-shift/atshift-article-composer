# Banner Candidates, Not Approved Assets

Superseded copy proposal. The latest English-only candidate is in `../v2/`.
Japanese banner artwork was declined and removed from the tracked candidates;
it is not a release deliverable. This does not affect Japanese plugin translations.

These images are proposals only. The owner explicitly requested that they not be
treated as the final design. Do not copy them into `wordpress-org/assets/`, upload
them to SVN, or attach them as final release branding without design approval.

Created with the built-in image generation tool on November 17, 2026. The visual
references were the owner's existing article-layout/AI icon and the atshift
Members directory banner. They are not screenshots of the plugin interface.

## Copy

English:

- atshift Article Composer
- Focus on your message.
- Let your AI compose and publish.

## Files

- `article-composer-banner-en-master.png`: original English generated master.
- `banner-1544x500.png` and `banner-772x250.png`: English candidate exports.

The exports are mechanically resized from their own language master. Approval
must cover composition, lettering, legibility and any final crop at both sizes.
The directory's [asset requirements](https://developer.wordpress.org/plugins/wordpress-org/plugin-assets/)
specify 772 x 250 and 1544 x 500 banners and support locale-specific filenames.

## Design Brief Used

Wide WordPress plugin banner; warm near-white background; clear left-aligned
atshift Article Composer heading and two-line benefit copy; restrained teal
divider; existing tactile article-layout icon with orange AI badge on the right.
Preserve the product icon's identity, readable text and generous margins. Follow
the visual hierarchy of the atshift Members reference, not its product wording.
